How to fix "Mismatched currency in shipping information" on Shopify

When Shopify Markets or a multi-currency feed app sends a product price in one currency (for example, USD for the US) while Merchant Center reads a shipping service in your store's base currency (for example, AUD, CAD, EUR, or GBP), Google flags Mismatched currency in shipping information and disapproves or limits the product.

1. Check whether shipping comes from the offer or the account

Open the affected product in Google Merchant Center under Products and inspect the final processed attributes. If an item-level shipping attribute is present, it overrides account-level shipping settings for that country. Check which data source (such as the Google & YouTube app, a supplemental feed, or a third-party feed app) wrote the currency.

2. Compare against live Shopify checkout for the same country

Open the exact product variant in a private browser window, switch to the target country, enter a valid postal code (e.g. ZIP 10001 for US), and read the exact shipping currency and amount at checkout. Never compare a default store-currency cart against a target-country Merchant Center offer.

3. Isolate one offer before editing account-wide shipping

Use the free browser checker to compare one disapproved offer against checkout and record the current data source before making changes. Once you fix the single writer causing the currency mismatch, wait for the next scheduled feed sync and verify the processed offer in Merchant Center.
