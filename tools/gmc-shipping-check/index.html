Case evidence

Current offer

In Merchant Center, open the affected item under Products. Copy its offer ID and target country. Use a two-letter country code such as US.

Merchant Center values

Use the price shown for this item. Enter amounts with a decimal point and three-letter currencies, for example 50.00 and USD. Enter 0 for free shipping.

Check shipping on the item or in Shipping and returns. Confirm applicability only when the rate covers this offer, country and delivery conditions.

Shopify checkout

Before placing the order, record the shipping price at checkout for the same variant, destination, service and cart conditions.
