Shopify Additional Scripts deprecated: fix missing or duplicate Google Ads purchase events

When Shopify stores migrate off legacy Thank You page Additional Scripts to Checkout Extensibility and Customer Events, Google Ads and GA4 purchase tracking often breaks in one of three ways: zero events fired, duplicate conversions from overlapping apps and custom pixels, or wrong conversion value/currency.

1. Confirm checkout_completed fires once

Open Settings > Customer events in Shopify, launch Shopify Pixel Helper, and run one authorized test order. Confirm whether checkout_completed fires once on the Thank You page. If it does not appear, check active custom pixel permissions and customer privacy consent rules before editing Google Ads.

2. Check for double-counting between the Google & YouTube app and Custom Pixels

A common post-migration bug occurs when both the official Google & YouTube app and a GTM Custom Pixel fire a purchase event for the same test order with different transaction_id formats (for example, Shopify order ID vs. order number #1001) or to two different send_to labels. Google Ads deduplicates only within the same conversion action and exact matching transaction ID.

3. Verify value, currency, and send_to in one test checkout

Capture the outgoing payload in Tag Assistant or GA4 DebugView and paste the redacted JSON into our free browser checker to verify exact transaction ID, currency, value basis (tax/shipping), and destination label.
